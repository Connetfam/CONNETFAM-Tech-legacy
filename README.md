ConnetFam 🌳
Building technology that keeps families connected.

ConnetFam is a technology company focused on helping families preserve their stories, memories, heritage, and connections across generations.

Our first product is Legacy — a family-focused platform being built to make it easier for families to keep their history and important memories together.

🌳 Legacy
Legacy is being developed as a digital family record and memory platform.

The vision is to help families:

Preserve family stories and memories
Build and explore family relationships
Keep important moments across generations
Preserve family heritage and culture
Organize photos and other family media
Create a lasting family record for future generations
🚧 Current Stage
Legacy is currently in MVP development.

The project is being built and tested publicly as we work toward getting the first families to use the product and provide feedback.
